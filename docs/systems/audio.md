# System: Audio

## Why it's a pillar, not an afterthought

The most underweighted thing in a wonder-and-discovery game. Sound carries half the
atmosphere and punches far above its cost. Put it in the PRD as a pillar. Painful to
bolt on late.

## Three roles (do all three)

1. **Ambient generative soundscapes per world** — the hush of an alien place; shifts
   with sensor mode (each sense should *sound* different, not just look different).
2. **Emotional score reserved for key beats** — discovery moments, the Earth reveal.
   Restraint makes the big moments land; wall-to-wall music deadens them.
3. **Sound as gameplay** — life carries *audio signatures*, so sound becomes part of
   detection, not just decoration. A sensor locking onto life has a tone; different
   life sounds different.

## The single most important sonic moment

(To decide — see open decisions.) Strong candidate: the tone/texture shift the instant
you switch to the right sense and hidden life resolves into view. If sight and sound
bloom together at that beat, the north-star moment doubles in power.

## Practical

Budget one good composer or a quality library *early*. Achievable solo or with
affordable help. For the slice, even a small but intentional sound pass on the
discovery beat will do disproportionate work in playtests.
